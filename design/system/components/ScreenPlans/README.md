# ScreenPlans
What the user knows **before** paying (section 06, Lock L39):

- **Offres** — DokTool Pro monthly or annual for verified doctors and residents: what it includes, what it does **not** include (stated plainly: no drug database, no writing or printing of prescriptions and certificates), duration, price in DZD with taxes, access level. No ribbons, countdowns or fake scarcity. Students are told they need no subscription.
- **Récapitulatif** — plan, price, period, access, payment method, renewal mode, and one acknowledgement: *« J’ai compris ce que comprend DokTool Pro. Le paiement n’est pas remboursable. »* The pay button is disabled until it is ticked.
- The checkout is reachable only when professional verification is VERIFIED; before that the plan button reads "Vérification requise" and links to the status.

Prices shown are illustrative — the plan catalogue and prices are open question Q3 of the Architecture Audit.

States: role-ineligible (student) · verification required · provider error (provider message + retry / other method) · bank transfer pending (neutral) · success (ScreenSubscriptionStates).
