# ScreenHomeStudent
Student Home. Same structure as the doctor's; the quick actions lead with Calculateurs. Student-tier content is available; professional-only items stay **visible and locked** in place with a lock and the word "Médecins", and one neutral sentence explains why — no purchase button (students are not a paying audience), no red, no modal (Locks L38, L39). A single row offers the profile transition (student → resident → doctor) with everything kept.

What a student can open is decided by the backend entitlement policy (section 27), never by a `professionalType == STUDENT` check in the app. States: student verification pending (neutral line; tools available within the pending window) · pending window elapsed (items locked with "Vérification requise").
